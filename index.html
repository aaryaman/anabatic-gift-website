Profile

Anabatic GIFT IFSC LLP (the “FME”) is a limited liability partnership in the International Financial Services Centre at GIFT City, Gujarat. It is registered with the International Financial Services Centres Authority (“IFSCA”) as an Authorised Fund Management Entity and undertakes fund management activities in the IFSC under the IFSCA (Fund Management) Regulations, 2025.

The information on this website relates solely to the FME’s activities in the IFSC, and is presented separately from any activities of its partners or of any group or affiliated entity.
